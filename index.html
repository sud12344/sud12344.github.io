What I did

  • Assisted in designing the deployment system, using Nitinol actuation and an origami flasher pattern to maximize stowage efficiency.
  • Designed spring-loaded hinge release mechanisms and structural housing to minimize mass-to-volume ratio and areal density.

Folding pattern and tests

  • The fold is a variation of the origami flasher pattern, inspired by Henning W. Scheel's 1974 patent, "Space-saving Storage of Flexible Sheets."
  • Folding was tested on central hubs of 6, 3 and 1.5 cm.
